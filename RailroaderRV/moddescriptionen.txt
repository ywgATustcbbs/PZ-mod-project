[h1]🚂 Railroader RV - Travel and Living[/h1]
[b]Turn a Railroader locomotive or railcar into a home that travels along the rails![/b]

Have you ever wondered what it would be like to have a home that can travel along the railroad in a post-apocalyptic world?

Now you can turn a locomotive or railcar into your own RV! Build independent water and electrical systems inside, then rest, store supplies, arrange furniture, grow crops, and raise livestock. You can also load your own car onto a flatcar, unload it when you reach a new city, and drive around to explore.

Whether you explore alone or survive with friends, you can drive your own mobile base through a zombie-infested world in search of your next place to settle.

[h2]A Standalone RV Mod[/h2]
This standalone RV mod requires [b]Railroader[/b], but does not depend on other RV mods (such as Project RV Interior) and does not require an RV map. The server generates RV interiors procedurally as needed.

[list]
[*][b]Multiple Vehicle Types:[/b] Supports locomotives, boxcars, and other Railroader vehicles in the future, with a separate RV space for each vehicle.
[*][b]Server Authority:[/b] Clients submit action requests. The server validates permissions, location, and the current world state before making changes to the world.
[/list]

[h2]Enterable Locomotive and Railcar Interiors[/h2]
Your vehicle is no longer just transportation. It can be a real, livable mobile base!

[list]
[*][b]Areas with Different Permissions:[/b] RV interiors are divided by a template into no-entry, non-buildable, and buildable areas.
[list]
[*][b]No-Entry Areas:[/b] You cannot enter or build in these areas. If you move beyond the walkable area, the server returns you to a valid position inside the RV. Template structures are protected, and buildings in non-buildable areas are also handled according to the protection rules.
[*][b]Non-Buildable Areas:[/b] You can pass through, drop items on the floor, and do ordinary activities, but you cannot build or dismantle buildings.
[*][b]Buildable Areas:[/b] You can build, dismantle, and arrange furniture and living facilities.
[/list]
[*][b]Independent Interior Space:[/b] Enter your own living area from a locomotive or railcar.
[*][b]Arrange It Your Way:[/b] Place furniture, storage containers, and other living facilities in areas where building is allowed. If the cab feels too small, a sandbox option can open the mechanical and electrical areas for building. This sacrifices some realism to give you more space to arrange things.
[*][b]RV Safehouse:[/b] You can claim the RV area as a safehouse. Claiming it requires the RV's water and electrical systems to be operating normally.
[/list]

[h2]Independent Water and Electrical Systems[/h2]
Life on the move still needs water and electricity! The systems aim to simulate how a real RV works while keeping gameplay in mind. You will need to build and maintain the water and electrical equipment inside your RV.

[h3]⚡ Electrical System[/h3]
[list]
[*]Generate electricity with different equipment: install a fuel-powered generator, or put solar panels and wind turbines on the roof to generate power from sunlight and wind.
[*]Store electricity in batteries. The RV uses a simplified [b]power generation → battery charging → battery storage → inverter powering appliances[/b] model, balancing realism and gameplay.
[*]Manage the power load from appliances and equipment inside the RV, and check their status and power use through the RV management panel.
[*]The system tracks power in W (watts) and energy in Wh (watt-hours). Each device has its own generation, consumption, and storage data.
[*]Circuit breakers provide overload protection. Without a circuit breaker, an overload may burn out the inverter.
[/list]

[h3]💧 Water System[/h3]
[list]
[*]Store water for daily use in onboard tanks and refill them manually as needed.
[*]The plumbing needs an electrically powered pressure pump to maintain water pressure. If the RV's electrical system cannot supply power, the water supply stops too.
[*]Install rain collectors on the roof through the RV management interface. Place collectors as needed; the roof does not come covered with collectors in advance.
[*]Install a water pump to draw water from nearby natural sources, such as rivers and lakes, into the RV tank.
[*]Contaminated water must be filtered before use. Automatically collected rainwater also needs to be filtered before it counts as usable water. Filters wear out over time, so check their condition and replace them when needed.
[*]Check water levels, equipment, and water supply status at any time through the RV management panel.
[/list]

Plan your resources carefully to make life on the move last longer!

[h2]🚃 Flatcars and Vehicle Transport[/h2]
Railcars can be converted into flatcars for transporting real vehicles.

[list]
[*]Convert a railcar that is not bound to an RV into a flatcar, then craft a loading ramp.
[*]Select a nearby vehicle to load, or select a vehicle on the flatcar to unload. You must carry a loading ramp to load or unload vehicles.
[*]Due to game engine limitations, loading deletes the original vehicle (changing its vehicle ID). Unloading creates a vehicle in the same state at the destination, but with a new vehicle ID. Some vehicle state that cannot be read cannot be saved. When loaded, the vehicle's keys and items in its containers drop to the ground and are not put into the new vehicle when it is unloaded.
[*]Vehicles on a flatcar are only visual models and do not match the actual vehicles. Third-party vehicle models may also appear in the wrong position or at the wrong scale. These visual differences do not mean your vehicle is damaged.
[/list]

[i]Note: Transporting vehicles on a flatcar will cause issues with mods that rely on vehicle IDs for their functionality, such as Project RV Interior and other mods that use vehicle IDs to link vehicles to their RVs.[/i]

[hr][h2]👥 Multiplayer[/h2]
Drive a locomotive with your friends and share the living space along the way. RV interactions are validated and handled by the server. If you run into compatibility issues, feedback is welcome.

[i]Multiplayer has been tested extensively and confirmed playable. Single-player has had only limited testing and is playable, but the game itself may cause single-player behavior to differ from multiplayer. Please report any issues.[/i]

[h2]Frequently Asked Questions[/h2]

[h3]1. Why does the screen go black when I enter an RV?[/h3]

This is normal.

The first time you enter an RV, the mod needs to [b]generate the RV interior procedurally[/b]. Before the RV can be generated, the corresponding map chunks must be loaded. Project Zomboid mods cannot force a specific chunk to load, so the player must first go there to trigger the game's chunk generation and loading. The mod temporarily teleports you to [b]Z = -15[/b] underground at the RV's location, then waits for the chunks to load and the RV to be generated.
[list]
[*][b]Generation succeeds:[/b] You are automatically teleported into the RV interior.
[*][b]Generation fails:[/b] You are automatically teleported back beside the locomotive.
[*][b]The black screen lasts a long time:[/b] This is usually because a slow server takes longer to load the map chunks and generate the RV interior.
[/list]
[b]This wait usually only happens the first time you enter an RV.[/b] Once the interior has been generated, you can enter again without generating it again, and the black screen will not appear.
